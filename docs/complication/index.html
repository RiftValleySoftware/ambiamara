RiVal.T Watch Complication

The complication reads the most recent timer sample for the selected host. WidgetKit displays the countdown from its deadline and changes the symbol or tint when the timer enters its warning or final phase.

The Watch app shares timer samples and timeline confirmations through the app group. When contact is lost, the complication can return to the configured ready duration.
